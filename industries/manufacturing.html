Edge AI for Smart Manufacturing.

Eliminate production floor downtime and empower your assembly line operators with instant, offline access to complex equipment schematics. Achieve zero-latency decision making entirely disconnected from the cloud.

Securing the Supply Chain

Modern manufacturing facilities consist of thousands of automated components, robotic arms, and legacy PLC systems. When an assembly line halts, root-cause diagnosis often requires sifting through hundreds of pages of disparate OEM unindexed PDF manuals—while production quotas slip by the minute.

Steel.vision brings offline RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) directly to the factory floor. By utilizing ruggedized Apple Silicon hardware, technicians can query technical queries through natural language—or directly overlay an AR lens onto the failing machine—to instantly pinpoint fault codes and exact procedural fixes without touching a corporate network.

Minimizing MTTR (Mean Time to Repair)

Relying on Tier 3 engineering support for every line stoppage is economically unsustainable. By democratizing specialized knowledge through multimodal AI, Tier 1 operators are elevated to expert troubleshooters. The system visually guides them through part replacement and recalibration workflows using 3D spatial mapping.

Manufacturing Core Benefits

  • Air-Gapped IP Protection: Trade secrets and proprietary production methods never leave the physical building.
  • Rapid Onboarding: Reduce training cycles from months to days by providing new hires with an algorithmic 'expert on their shoulder'.
  • Automated Change Over: Guide operators through complex tooling changeovers visually, reducing errors and setup time.
